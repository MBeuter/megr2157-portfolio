## Task A:  Portfolio Analysis

Portfolio 1: https://nhoong.github.io/

  a.  Navigability 
  The engineering portfolio is navigable in under 60 seconds. It is concise in content and sectioned off by the individual content.

  b.  Reproducibility
  Only some projects have a dimensional drawing, so not all projects are reproduceable. There is room for improvement in the availability of information for some projects.

  c.  Evidence of reasoning
  The final results are shown for the individual designs without the corresponding reasonining. However, Hoong's capstone project includes bullet points with process improvements and methodical reasoning.

  d.  Professional tone  
  The tone used in the portfolio is appropriate in syntax and vocabulary. The sentences are grammatically correct, and Hoong uses a variety of words to describe his approach to engineering. This language is appropriate to hand to an employer.


Portfolio 2: https://sites.google.com/view/sethschafferportfolio/home

  a.  Navigability
  The engineering portfolio is very thorough and navigation is possible, but not quick due to the amount of content.
  
  b.  Reproducibility
  Schaffer provides videos with some of the processes for the creation of his projects, but there are no dimensional drawings. One would have to inquire about further details in this case.
  
  c.  Evidence of reasoning
  There are some chronological tellings alongside the descriptions of the projects as well as set-backs. He describes the circumstances of design, but not the thinking process.
  
  d.  Professional tone
  The tone used by Schaffer is more on the informal side. He uses colloquial words such as "amazing" and tells the circumstances of his design like a story, rather than a report.
